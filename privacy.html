Privacy

Ymatch is an early student project run by one person. Last updated: October 5, 2026.

What we collect

  • Waitlist: your BYU email (the waitlist is for BYU students only), first name (optional), gender, school year, and which flyer or link you came from.
  • Matching survey (pilot): your answers, your BYU email, your phone number if you give one, your age, and who you're looking for.

Who can see it

Only the organizer (one person). Other users never see your answers or contact information.

What a match sees

If you're matched, the other person sees your first name, a compatibility score, and a few answers you have in common. Your email and phone number are shared only if you both say yes.

How it's used

To pick matches and to improve how matching works. We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for anything unrelated to Ymatch.

Where it's stored

In Google Forms and Google Sheets, and in a Supabase database. These are third-party services with their own security and privacy practices.

Delete your data

Email spmadsen21@gmail.com and ask, and your information will be deleted. You can leave the pilot at any time. To report a problem with a match, use the same address.

Who can join

You must be 18 or older.

Not affiliated

Ymatch is a student project. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by BYU or The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.
